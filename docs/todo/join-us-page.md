# /join-us/

Love-hate League of Legends? Passionate about processing and interpreting data? Have experience with coding? Join us!
You don't need to be experienced, but not a complete beginner either.

## You should have experience with

- Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML/CSS, Git, GitHub, SQL
- You are keeping up with AI trends and can code with AI agent effectively and responsibly
- And that's it from the technicalities

## Nice to have experience with

- FastAPI/Flask/Django, React, Next.js/Node.js, PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy, ORM
- VSCode (or other IDE), GitHub Copilot (or other AI agent), DbVisualizer (or other DB client)
- Linux, Chrome DevTools, DigitalOcean, CI/CD, SSH
- QA, JIRA, Confluence, Slack

## The real deal-breaker

- You are passionate about League of Legends and data analytics/data science
- You can work both independently and as part of a team
- You have will to learn, not giving up easily, thinking of solutions rather than excuses
- You are open and communicative, there is no such thing as a "dumb question"
- You focus on the process rather than the outcome
- You have creative mind and can bring your own ideas to this project
- You can dedicate ~10+ hours a week on average

If most of the text above suits you, you might be a great fit.

## Why to join us

- Great internship opportunity (you will need to sign Internship Agreement)
- Work with experienced developer, from whom you can learn and gain valuable experience
- Get better in all technologies mentioned above and more, including personal growth
- Analyze League of Legends players with ease, see what other sites won't show you
- Possibility of a profit in the future, though this shouldn't be your motivation

Note that the project is currently non-profit and only long-term (3+ months) contributors are welcomed.
